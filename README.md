# USA-Regional-Sales-Analysis
A multi-page Power BI dashboard analyzing $1.00B in U.S. sales across executive trends, product performance, channel efficiency, and geographic insights. Designed with a dark theme and interactive visuals to support strategic decision-making and stakeholder storytelling.

## 📦 Features

- **Executive Overview**: Tracks $1.00B revenue, $462M profit, 37.36% margin, and 64K orders with $19.28K revenue per order.
- **Monthly Trends**: Visualizes revenue ($90M–$100M) and profit ($35M–$40M) seasonality across 12 months.
- **Customer Segmentation**: Maps order-value tiers, highlighting ~1,000 orders below $0.5M.
- **Pricing Efficiency**: Analyzes unit price vs. margin, identifying high-efficiency bands between $2K–$5K with up to 60% margin.
- **Product Performance**: Ranks top 9 products by revenue (Product 26 ~ $0.1B) and margin (Product 9 ~ 50%).
- **Channel Scorecard**: Compares Wholesale, Distributor, and Export routes by revenue, profit, and margin per sale.
- **Geographic Insights**: Highlights Midwest as top region ($372.1M, 30.1%) and maps state-level profit distribution.
- **Risk Signals**: Flags bottom 5 customers and states for churn mitigation and growth targeting.

## 📊 Visuals Used

- KPI cards
- Line charts
- Bar charts
- Scatter plots
- Donut charts
- Interactive map

## 🧠 Business Impact

- Enabled margin optimization by identifying high-efficiency price bands and top-performing products.
- Supported channel strategy by quantifying margin per sale across routes.
- Informed regional planning with revenue and profit breakdowns by state and region.
- Delivered executive-ready insights with clean layout, dark theme, and modular navigation.
